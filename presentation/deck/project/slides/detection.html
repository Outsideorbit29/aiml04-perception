5 more epochs with 25 % CARLA frames: 0.624 → 0.819 on 600 held-out frames; BDD val 0.553 → 0.539

Time: 0:50. I fine-tuned YOLO11s from COCO weights. On the 10k BDD val images it reaches 0.553 mAP at 0.5 (W&B eval 8tzih6my). Compared fairly on the seven classes COCO shares with BDD, that is 0.560 against 0.361 zero-shot. The surprise came in CARLA. On 3,000 recorded frames the BDD model scored 0.530 and plain COCO 0.485, so most of the BDD gain did not transfer to rendered images; that +0.045 is probably an upper bound, because COCO calls CARLA riders “person” and those count as false positives. So I fine-tuned five more epochs on a mix with 25 % CARLA frames (W&B i6dnlk0c). On 600 held-out CARLA frames mAP went from 0.624 to 0.819; on BDD it dropped 0.014. Caveat: the held-out frames come from the same town and session, so this is an in-domain gain.
