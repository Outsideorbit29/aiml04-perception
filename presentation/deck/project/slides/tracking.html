Cost of the fix: recall 67.7 → 64.6, and a new id appears 2 frames (100 ms at 20 Hz) after the first detection. Detection + tracking: 52 FPS on a T4.

Time: 0:40. BDD’s tracking split is not on Kaggle, so I evaluated on eight KITTI tracking sequences (W&B qvrkp7t1). The default ByteTrack produced many one-frame ghost tracks. Requiring two consecutive matched frames before a track gets an id cut ID switches from 310 to 176 and raised IDF1 from 65.7 to 67.4; MOTA barely moved. The trade-off is slightly lower recall and a 2-frame delay before a new object gets an id — 100 ms at CARLA’s 20 Hz, fine for braking at urban speeds. Pedestrians are much weaker than vehicles; part of that is a label mismatch, since KITTI cyclist boxes include the bike and BDD rider boxes do not. The settings were chosen on the same sequences, so these numbers are in-sample.
