Time: 0:45. The brief asked for a classical lane detector compared with a segmentation model. Both produce the same output: measured left and right boundaries and an ego-lane mask (W&B bf4xp164). On 500 BDD val images YOLOP finds the right lines far more often: F1 0.515 against 0.231, precision 0.60 against 0.24. The classical method is fooled by car edges, hood reflections and road text. The ego-lane IoU is a tie at about 0.42, because YOLOP returns no line at all on 129 of 500 images; when it predicts, its IoU is higher, 0.52 against 0.45. Night costs YOLOP little. Snow is its worst condition and curves are weak for both, on only 20 images.
