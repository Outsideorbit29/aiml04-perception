Data

Heavily skewed classes, kept all nine

  • BDD100K: 20,000-image train subset (stratified by time of day and weather) + full 10,000 val
  • Car is 55 % of boxes; motorcycle 239× rarer, rider 148×
  • Repeat-factor sampling: motorcycle ×1.75, rider ×1.36, bicycle ×1.24
  • KITTI: LiDAR distance ground truth + 8 tracking sequences; CARLA: 3,000 recorded frames

Boxes per class, log scale (docs/figures/eda_class_counts.png)

Time: 0:40. BDD100K was the main dataset. The full train split is 69,863 images with about 1.27 million boxes. To fit the GPU budget I took a 20,000-image subset, stratified by time of day and weather, and kept the full 10,000-image val split. About 40 % of images are at night and 55 % of boxes are small. The class balance is very skewed: cars are 55 % of all boxes. I kept all nine classes, because rider versus person matters for driving, and used repeat-factor sampling so images with rare classes appear more often. That added only 853 entries per epoch. KITTI gave LiDAR ground truth for distance and tracking sequences for MOT. Later I recorded 3,000 CARLA frames with ground truth on the laptop and uploaded them to Kaggle.
